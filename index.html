Good questions

A few things, clearly answered.

Is HexPay really free with 0% commission?

Yes. There are no setup fees, monthly charges or commission on payments. Optional add-ons may be offered in the future.

Can I use my personal FamPay @fam or @yesfam UPI ID?

Yes. HexPay is built to receive payments straight into your FamPay UPI ID. Funds go directly to your account.

How fast are payments verified?

Usually about a second after the alert mail reaches your inbox. The timing depends on how quickly that mail is delivered.

Which apps can customers pay with?

Any UPI app, including FamPay, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred and others. Customers can scan the QR or tap the deep link.

Is my Gmail password safe?

You connect a Google App Password, not your Google account password. HexPay stores the mailbox credential encrypted with AES-256-GCM. You can revoke an app password from your Google account.

Do I need GST or KYC to start?

HexPay itself does not ask for GST or KYC documents. You are responsible for following the rules that apply to your business.

Can I use HexPay with a Telegram bot or my website?

Yes. Connect through the REST API and signed webhooks. Examples are provided for PHP, Python, Node.js and cURL.

What if one UPI ID reaches its limit?

Add UPI accounts and set a daily limit for each. New orders can be spread across accounts that remain under their limit.
